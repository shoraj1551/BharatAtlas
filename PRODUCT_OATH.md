# BharatAtlas Product Oath

## Our Commitment to Truth and Trust

This document defines the ethical stance of BharatAtlas. Every developer, contributor, and maintainer must understand and uphold these principles.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. **Truth Over Completeness**
We will never fake completeness. Empty sections are acceptable. Unknown is a valid state.

### 2. **Transparency Over Persuasion**
Every piece of information declares its epistemic type. AI inferences never masquerade as facts.

### 3. **Uncertainty Over Certainty**
We show confidence levels, not absolute truths. Old data automatically loses confidence.

### 4. **Contradiction Over Consensus**
Conflicting data is shown, not hidden. Users see all versions, not our preferred one.

### 5. **Provenance Over Convenience**
Every fact traces to a source. No data point exists without attribution.

### 6. **Comparability Over Flexibility**
We prevent misleading comparisons. Metrics must align before comparison is allowed.

### 7. **Temporal Context Over Timelessness**
Every data point declares when it was last valid. Truth has a timestamp.

### 8. **Read-Only by Default**
Most users consume; few contribute. Default access is read-only.

### 9. **Human Over AI**
Human verification always wins. AI inferences are clearly labeled and visually distinct.

### 10. **Audit Over Opacity**
Every admin action is logged. Claims can be challenged with full traceability.

---

## What We Will NOT Do

- ❌ Hide unknowns or data gaps
- ❌ Present AI inferences as facts
- ❌ Allow comparisons without metric alignment
- ❌ Show normalized data without disclosure
- ❌ Display data without temporal context
- ❌ Suppress contradictory information
- ❌ Fake completeness to appear authoritative
- ❌ Allow unapproved insights to leak publicly
- ❌ Present single scores without qualitative context
- ❌ Enable mutations without audit trails

---

## What We WILL Do

- ✅ Declare knowledge type for every data point
- ✅ Attach confidence scores to every insight
- ✅ Show "Unknown" as a first-class state
- ✅ Display provenance in human-readable format
- ✅ Visually distinguish AI inferences from facts
- ✅ Enforce comparability contracts
- ✅ Disclose normalization clearly
- ✅ Mandate temporal validity
- ✅ Decay confidence as data ages
- ✅ Surface contradictions honestly
- ✅ Enable dispute with traceability
- ✅ Default to read-only access
- ✅ Summarize trust posture qualitatively
- ✅ Accept empty sections
- ✅ Maintain complete audit trails

---

## Developer Responsibilities

As a developer on BharatAtlas, you commit to:

1. **Never compromise epistemic clarity** for user experience
2. **Always validate knowledge type** declarations
3. **Enforce confidence scoring** on all insights
4. **Maintain audit trails** for all mutations
5. **Prevent unapproved content** from leaking
6. **Surface unknowns** explicitly
7. **Protect comparability** contracts
8. **Disclose normalization** transparently
9. **Respect temporal validity** requirements
10. **Uphold read-only defaults**

---

## Enforcement

This oath is enforced through:

- **Code reviews** - All PRs checked against these principles
- **Validation rules** - Schema validation enforces requirements
- **Automated tests** - Tests verify epistemic clarity
- **Audit logs** - All violations are traceable
- **Feature flags** - Safety valves for instant disabling

---

## Violation Consequences

Violations of this oath result in:

1. **Immediate code rejection** in review
2. **Reversion of changes** that compromise trust
3. **Documentation of incident** in audit log
4. **Team discussion** on preventing recurrence
